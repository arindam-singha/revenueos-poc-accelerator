# Cut list

The MVP intentionally leaves these items out to keep implementation focused:

- Authentication, multi-tenant access controls, and user/role management.
- Production deployment, containerization, hosted database, and operational telemetry.
- Integrations with CRM, calendar, ticketing, or customer data platforms.
- Automated discovery from uploaded files and richer semantic/vector retrieval.
- Editing, version history, collaboration, and lifecycle transitions for saved plans.
- Configurable SLA policies, notifications, and background escalation workflows.
- Streaming responses, provider failover, and advanced LLM evaluation/guardrails.

These are follow-on opportunities, not prerequisites for demonstrating the brief-to-plan workflow.
